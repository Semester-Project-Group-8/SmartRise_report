\section{Objective} The main objective of SmartRise is to automate the process of changing the height of smart desks and create convenience for the employees in the office. By automating different tasks, convenience is created for the different actors interacting with the solution.\par
The main actor the system is centered around is the employee sitting at the table. The objective for them is, when they get close to their table, their phone picks up the signal and sets the employee's preferred desk height before the employee arrives at the table. This automated system allows the tables to change positions throughout the day depending on the needs of the company. This assures that when the owner of the table comes to the office, it will be set to their liking. As the system is centered around them, every other automation process is overridden by the presence of an employee, as to not disturb them.\par
The preferred settings of each employee are saved and they are not tied to their table. This is useful in cases when someone is changing desks. The system allows the transfer of preferred settings from one table to another simply by the employee being assigned to another table. This further increases efficiency and comfort, as the need for them to manually set up their new table is eliminated.\par
Employees have access to the SmartRise mobile app, which is designed to work without frequent user interaction. The app allows them to view or change their settings even without them being at the desk.
The second actor this system is designed to help is the cleaning staff. When the office is scheduled for cleaning, the system is designed to set each table to its maximum height to ease cleaning around them. Cleaners have the possibility to manually raise the tables of an entire department, if they need to clean outside the set cleaning time.\par
Another main objective of the automation is for each table to be set to an optimal height, that creates an aesthetic workspace, outside of office hours. This ensures the company being viewed in a professional light by anyone passing by.\par
The system is tied together by the final actor, the administrator. Each department is managed by one administrator. They are responsible for setting up beacons, approving new users, setting default cleaning times, reviewing reported problems and reassigning users to tables. For convenience and increased overview, administrators have access to a separate web application, from where they can control the system.\par
